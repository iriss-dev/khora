Acoustic & Hardware Safety Waiver

Prototype exposure assessment and hardware interlock requirements.

Standards scope and unverified acoustic exposure

Focused 40 kHz ultrasound produces mechanical radiation pressure ($P = 2I/c$). Force and duty-cycle settings alone do not establish safe exposure. Calibrated acoustic measurements and a device-specific assessment are required; no OSHA/IEC certification is claimed.

24V DC / MOSFET High-Current Electrical Precautions

Switching 256 piezoelectric transducers draws up to 8–15A instantaneous peak currents. Builders must provide star-ground PCB layouts, thermal heatsinking, and emergency mechanical power disconnects.

50ms Hardware Watchdog Interlock Protocol

Robotic arms operating in co-presence with human hands in the DCRA holographic workspace must run a 50ms heartbeat watchdog. If optical tracking is lost for $\ge 50\text{ ms}$, joint velocities must halt immediately.
